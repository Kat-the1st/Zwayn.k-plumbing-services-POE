Our Roots:Where It All Started(2018)

The story of Zwaynk's Plumbing didnt start in a fancy office in Sandton.It started in a backyard in Central Park,Pretoria.One man, one hand toolbox brought from a pawn shop and an old toyota bakkie that broke down twice a week.

Our founder,Zwayn grew up in tembisa seeing the same problem over and over.A gogo's toilet would run non-stop for months, wasting thousands of litres and running her municipal bill to R3000 simply because she couldn't find an affordable,honest plumber who would come to Tembisa and not charge R1500 just to show up.Big plumbing companies only wanted big jobs in the surburbs.Local guys would come and make the problem worse.

In 2018,he decided to change that.He took his last savings, got certified in advanced geyser installation and leak detection as well as knocking door to door. The first 6 months he did 3 jobs a week. Sometimes for free, just to prove his work. Word of mouth is powerful in the kasi. One happy client in Hospital View told 3 more. By the end of 2019, we had a waiting list.

The problems We Solved For Gauteng

We discovered three major problems facing homeowners in Gauteng and we built our entire company to solve:

1.The Price Scare: Clients were scared to call a plumber because they didn't know what they would be charged.We solved it with our Upfront Qoute System - No work starts until you approve the price.

2.The No-Show Plumber: You take off work and wait the whole day but nobody comes. We solved it with our 60-minute Response Promise and live Whatsapp tracking.When we say 10am,that's the time we arrive.

3.The Temporary Fix:Many plumbera use cheap,non-SABS materials that leak again in two months.We solved it by using ONLY SABS approved pipes,valves and fittings while we back every single job with a written 12-MONTH WORKMANSHIP GUARANTEE. If it leaks again due to our work,we fix it for Free.

Our Growth:From One ToolBox To A Trusted Brand(2018-2026)

Phase 1: The Hustle(2018-2020)

we focused on the basucs, General repairs,leaking taps,tolet repairs and blocked drains. We learned every pipe layout in Tembisa's households, bonded and newly built homes.We earned a reputation for the guys who could unblock a drain without making a mess in your house.

Phase 2: The Specialization(2020-2022)

During the 2020 lockdown, bursst geysers became a crisis.Families were home 24/7,geysers were overworked and no one was availabe. We stayed open as an essential service provider. we invested in advanced geyser training,thermal leak detectors and high pressure jetting machines. We completed over 250 geysers onstallations and repairs in 3 years alone, saving clients millions in water damage.

Phase 3:The Expansion(2022-2025)

We could no longer handle the demand alone. We hired and trained 3 more qualified plumbers from Tembisa.We bought our second service van and we expanded over coverage from just Tembisa to all of Johannesburg - Roodepoort, Randburg, Fourways, MIdrand and East Rand. We launched our Landlord & Property Management division, now trusted over 40 landlords and 5 letting agencies.

Phase 4: Today(2026)

Today, Zwaynk plumbing stands as one of the fastest growing independent plumbing brands in Gauteng. We operate a full fleet, we have a dedicated bookings pffice and we still maintain the same kasi values we started with.
